📥 Load ReptiCube Defaults

Adds the 22 default materials and 14 SKU BOMs (RC30 → RCA180) sourced from your costing sheet. Safe: existing materials and products will not be overwritten — only missing ones are added. Products that exist but have no BOM yet will have their default BOM filled in.

📥 Load PinkFoot Defaults

Adds 38 materials (boards, fasteners, finishes, consumables, packaging) and 42 products across Compactum / Cot / Rocking Chair / Foot stool categories, sourced from your costing sheet and updated catalogue. Per-product BOMs and cutlists start empty — fill them in via the BOM tab as you have time, or send a separate costing sheet for each product.
Safe: existing materials and products are not overwritten — only missing ones are added (deduped by name).

🔄 Reconcile Catalogue with Defaults

Brings your live data in line with the latest defaults for the active biz. In one pass: renames migrated products (and the orders linked to them), moves products to updated categories (Feroche → Sofa, Feroche Chair → Chair on Bellville), adds missing products, and flags products no longer in defaults for deletion. Snapshot-backed — single Ctrl+Z reverts the whole operation. You'll see a preview with counts and exact rename/removal lists before anything is applied.
