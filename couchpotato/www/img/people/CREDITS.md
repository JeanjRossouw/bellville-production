## Swapping in your own photos

Real photos of your own floor beat stock every time. Save each one over the
file of the same name, at roughly the same shape, in both formats:

- hero-wide.jpg: landscape 4:3, 1600 x 1200; keep the person in the middle or right
  (the left side fades into black behind the headline)
- hero-tall.jpg: portrait 9:16, 900 x 1600; keep the top and bottom fairly dark,
  the headline sits at the top and the buttons at the bottom
- cut, sew, upholster, dispatch: portrait 4:5, 720 x 900
- office.jpg: landscape 4:3, 1200 x 900
- cnc.jpg: wide 21:9, 2000 x 857 (it sits under a dark overlay)

Make a .webp of each at the same size (the page offers WebP first and falls
back to the .jpg). Keep each file under about 250 KB.
