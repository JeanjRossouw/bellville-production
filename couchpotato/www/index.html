What it does

Everything between the order and the invoice.

Built with working South African furniture makers, for the way an upholstery and furniture factory actually runs.

Orders

Every order, where it is and when it is due.

Capture an order once and follow it from new to dispatched. Nothing gets lost between the showroom, the office and the floor.

  • Your own order numbers, with each customer's reference kept alongside
  • Due dates, late warnings and a fabric status on every order
  • A history on each order of who changed what, and when
  • Two people can work at the same time without overwriting each other
Factory floor

Plan the weeks ahead, print the job cards.

A four-week planner shows what is being built when. Drag an order into the week it gets built, or tap it on a phone.

  • Open, due-this-week, late and waiting-on-fabric at a glance
  • A fabric list of everything still to order or receive
  • Printable job cards, each with a QR code for scanning out
  • One tap marks a piece in production or done
Costing

Know what a couch really costs you.

Materials, labour and a fair share of your overheads add up to the true cost of each piece, and the margin you make on it.

  • A materials list with current prices; change one and every product updates
  • A bill of materials and labour hours per product
  • Rent, wages and running costs spread across what you make each month
  • A printable price list for customers and a cost sheet for you
Showroom till

Sell from the floor or to order, in one sale.

A point of sale for the tablet at the counter, using the same products and prices as the factory.

  • Made-to-order lines go straight to the factory as orders
  • From-stock lines take the piece off your showroom count
  • Every sale raises an invoice with the payment already on it
  • Discounts and VAT worked out for you
Scan out

Scan it at the door. The customer hears about it.

Point any phone at the QR code on the job card as a piece leaves. It is marked dispatched, with who and when.

  • Works with the phone's own camera; no scanner to buy
  • One tap sends the customer a WhatsApp or email that it is on its way
  • Dispatched pieces queue up for invoicing so nothing goes unbilled
Invoicing

Everything dispatched gets billed.

Invoices in your name and numbering, with your bank details and VAT when you are registered.

  • Invoice all of a customer's dispatched orders in one go
  • Record payments and see who owes what
  • Statements per customer
  • An export of every invoice line for your accountant

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. Sign up with your company name, email and a password. You only pay if you decide to carry on after 14 days.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you have not subscribed, the app becomes read only. You and your team can still sign in and see everything you captured, but you cannot add or change anything until you subscribe. Nothing is deleted.

How do I pay?

Monthly, through PayFast, by card or instant EFT. The owner subscribes under Settings, Billing, and PayFast takes the payment each month.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, under Settings, Billing. No more payments are taken, and you keep full use until the end of the month you have paid for.

Who can see my data?

Only the people you invite. Each business is kept separate in the database itself, so no other company on the app can see your orders, customers, prices or costs.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install. The till is made for a tablet at the counter, and scan out uses the phone's own camera.

How many people can use it?

As many as you need. The price is the same whether you have two people or twenty.
